# ADR-0010: Multi-company boundaries and consolidated reporting

- **Status:** Accepted for the management-report phase · 2026-11. Statutory translation and elimination accounting remain open.
- **Context:** A tenant owns its own users, roles, sessions, modules, settings, business records, events, audit trail, and RLS scope. Typed references and row policies deliberately prevent a record in one tenant from pointing at records in another. A branch is a location inside a tenant and does not provide legal-entity isolation. Consent-based groups, per-user trial-balance grants, and a first read-only group trial balance are implemented; FX policy and intercompany elimination remain limited.
- **Decision:** Keep the tenant as the legal-entity security and accounting boundary. Introduce a separate company-group relationship only after the member tenant explicitly consents. Do not replace tenant-scoped record keys with a shared company identifier or cross-tenant foreign keys.
- **Access model:** Group membership grants no access by itself. A group administrator grants named active owner-tenant users access to a specific report family. A member tenant administrator can revoke consent. Every consolidated request rechecks the active grant, group membership, reciprocal consent, caller permission, and member status. Revocation takes effect on the next request.
- **Data access:** Keep the current app.tenant_id RLS policy active for every organization query. A consolidation service may open separate read-only tenant transactions and combine bounded report results in application memory. It must never issue an unscoped query across business tables or broaden an ordinary user's tenant context.
- **Accounting behavior:** The management trial balance includes each entity's source amount and currency, translated amount, conversion rate, effective date and rate age, plus the group reporting currency. It uses the latest direct or inverse rate on or before the report end date for every balance column and fails closed when a converted member has no rate-age policy or its quote is older than the group-configured maximum. It does not eliminate intercompany balances. Users can save an immutable, audited payload of a report; opening the saved payload still requires the caller's active grant. This is not a statutory translation policy. Any elimination entries must be separate, attributable accounting records with their own approval and audit trail; reporting must not silently rewrite member ledgers.
- **Delivery order:**
  1. **Done:** Add consent-based group membership and revocation, with audit events in both affected tenants.
  2. **Done for trial balance:** Add named-user report grants and a read-only consolidated trial balance. A consolidated balance sheet remains future work.
  3. **In progress:** Source and translated amounts, rate and effective date, immutable audited trial-balance snapshots, and an explicit per-group rate-age policy are implemented; missing, invalid, or stale rates fail closed. Formal reconciliation controls remain open.
  4. **Not started:** Add intercompany matching and elimination journals as a separate workflow.
- **Required verification:** Test that unconsented, revoked, suspended, and deleted member tenants are not readable; users without an active group report grant cannot access live or saved reports; saved report payloads cannot be modified or deleted through generic APIs; tenant RLS still isolates every member query; malformed, missing, policy-less, or over-age FX rates fail closed; and every consolidated amount reconciles to the entity-level details.
- **Review when:** Product requirements identify applicable accounting standards and the rate policy for closing balances, period movements, and equity. The current group model permits a tenant to join multiple groups; add a parent-company constraint only if product rules require it.
